# Heroic AI Product

Heroic AI is the product and primary user journey. Research, extraction, comparison, memory, model execution and evaluation are supporting capabilities.

The overnight research workflow exists to improve those capabilities through bounded, auditable evidence. Its outputs are observations and candidate improvement signals, not production authority.

See `NIGHTLY_RESEARCH_RUNBOOK.md` for the 01:00-09:00 IST execution window, 24-program coverage contract and evidence rules.
