# Cross-Fire Family Method

Foundation participates in Cross-Fire as a public-safe evidence lane, not as a private policy or promotion authority.

The canonical family contract remains the authority for ownership and placement. Cross-Fire adds independent evidence only:

- forward contract-to-implementation
- reverse consumer-to-owner
- boundary/security
- evidence/regression
- policy-to-side-effect
- side-effect/recovery

Up to six disjoint lanes may inspect the family in parallel. Identical searches are not independent coverage.

Deterministic checks run first: exact hashes, structural fingerprints, dependency/ownership indexes, and policy overlap. AI providers may supply candidate findings for ambiguous/high-similarity cases, but model agreement never becomes policy, acceptance, merge, or deployment authority.

Read-only inspection may be parallel. Mutations and merges remain serialized through the canonical owner. Do not create a second scheduler, provider router, ledger, policy engine, or evidence authority.
